    How clans work

    • Tags are 1 to 5 letters or numbers
    • Up to 10 members, one clan per player
    • Invite players, kick, hand over ownership or disband
    • Only multiplayer time counts, and AFK farms count too
    • Playtime is never capped per player

    On your Discord profile

    On multiplayer your status swaps between your FPS and your clan.

    Bunny Client Playing on mc.example.net FPS: 144

    Every 15 seconds in game. This demo is faster. Both the server and clan lines can be turned off.

    Good to know. Clans, badges and capes use the Bunny Client network and need a real Microsoft account. Everything else works offline. The network stores your Minecraft UUID and username, your clan, your multiplayer playtime and your equipped cape. Your login token goes to Mojang only.

    Install it in a minute

    Four steps, then press Right Shift in game.

    1. 1
      Install Fabric

      Run the Fabric Installer for Minecraft 1.21.11.

    2. 2
      Add Fabric API

      Download Fabric API and put it in your .minecraft/mods folder.

    3. 3
      Download Bunny Client

      Get the latest .jar from the Releases page.

    4. 4
      Drop it in and launch

      Move the .jar into .minecraft/mods and start the Fabric profile. Press Right Shift to open the menu.

    What's new

    Every release, newest first.

    v1.1.0 October 4, 2026 Latest

    Clans, capes, mod options, a redesigned menu and a batch of new mods.

    Clans and capes

    • Clans. Create a clan, invite players, kick, hand over ownership or disband. Tags are 1 to 5 letters or numbers, with 12 tag colors, up to 10 members and one clan per player.
    • Weekly and all-time leaderboards. The top 10 clans, switchable between all time and weekly. The weekly board resets every Monday at 00:00 UTC and shows last week's champion.
    • Clan tags on nametags. Your tag shows after the bunny badge, in your clan's color.
    • Weekly Champion cape. Every member of the #1 clan with at least 30 minutes of playtime gets a gold cape for the following week. Playtime is not capped.
    • Cosmetics screen to equip or remove capes. The Bunny team and beta testers have their own capes.
    • Verified login. The mod signs in through Mojang, so UUIDs can't be faked. Network features need a real Microsoft account.
    • Discord status now alternates between your FPS and your clan on multiplayer, with an option to turn the clan line off.

    Menu and options

    • Right-click any mod for its options. Entity Hitboxes lets you pick which entities get a box. Custom Crosshair has style, size, thickness, gap, color and outline. Zoom, Entity Culling, Particle Limiter, Freelook and Discord have options too.
    • Every HUD module can change its background, border, text color and label color.
    • A new look. A sidebar with icons, a searchable mod grid with category filters, rounded animated controls, and a new title screen.

    New mods

    • HUD: Combo Counter, Reach Display, Speed, Item Counter, Clock and Memory Usage.
    • Freelook. Hold a key to look around while your aim stays put.
    • Clear Weather. Hide rain and thunder on your screen only.

    Fixed

    • Fullbright now works without a night vision potion and puts your brightness back when you turn it off.
    • Entity Culling, Particle Limiter and Custom Crosshair now actually do something. Before, their switches changed a setting that nothing used.
    • Zoom FOV is respected, and zoom eases back out after you let go.
    v1.0.1 October 1, 2026

    Fixed

    • Entity hitboxes no longer crash the game and display correctly.
    • Toggle Sprint sprints for you again. Toggle Sneak works too.
    • Bunny badge now shows only beside players who use Bunny Client, instead of everyone. It uses a custom icon, because the old emoji never showed in Minecraft's font.

    Added

    • A small server so the mod knows who else uses it, which updates about every 20 seconds.
    • Colored badges for the team: red for the owner, purple for developers, aqua for beta testers.
    • A redesigned mod menu with search and categories, and a new title screen.
