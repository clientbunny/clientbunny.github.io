🐰 Bunny Client

A standalone, Lunar Client‑inspired mod with Discord Rich Presence, quality‑of‑life overlays, and FPS improvements — all in one package.

⬇ Download Latest View Source

Free & open source · Built for Fabric · Minecraft 1.21.1

Features

Everything you need, built right in.

📈

FPS Counter

See your frames per second in real time with a clean, minimal on‑screen overlay.

📍

Coordinates

Always know where you are. X, Y, Z displayed neatly on your HUD at all times.

🎮

Discord Rich Presence

Custom Discord RPC integration — show off that you're playing with Bunny Client.

🛡️

Armor Status

Track your armor durability at a glance without opening your inventory.

🧪

Potion Status

Active potion effects displayed on your HUD so you never lose track of your buffs.

🖱️

CPS Counter

Monitor your clicks per second live — useful for PvP and click speed tracking.

🐰

Bunny Indicator

Players running Bunny Client get a 🐰 shown before their name so you always know who's in the crew.

Installation

Get up and running in under a minute.

  1. 1
    Install Fabric

    Download and run the Fabric Installer for Minecraft 1.21.1.

  2. 2
    Add Fabric API

    Download Fabric API and drop it into your .minecraft/mods folder.

  3. 3
    Download Bunny Client

    Grab the latest .jar from the Releases page.

  4. 4
    Drop it in mods & launch

    Move the .jar into your .minecraft/mods folder and launch Minecraft with the Fabric profile.
