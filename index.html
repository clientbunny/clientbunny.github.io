✨ Features

Everything you need, all built right in.

📈

FPS Counter

See your frames per second in real time with a clean on‑screen overlay.

📍

Coordinates

Always know where you are. X, Y, Z displayed neatly on your HUD.

🎮

Discord Rich Presence

Custom RPC integration — show the world you're playing with Bunny Client.

🛡️

Armor Status

Track your armor durability at a glance without opening your inventory.

🧪

Potion Status

Active potion effects on your HUD so you never lose track of your buffs.

🖱️

CPS Counter

Monitor your clicks per second live — great for PvP and tracking speed.

🐰

Bunny Indicator

Bunny Client users get a 🐰 before their name so you know who's in the crew.

🌸 Installation

Get up and running in under a minute.

  1. 1
    Install Fabric

    Download and run the Fabric Installer for Minecraft 1.21.11.

  2. 2
    Add Fabric API

    Download Fabric API and drop it into your .minecraft/mods folder.

  3. 3
    Download Bunny Client

    Grab the latest .jar from the Releases page.

  4. 4
    Drop it in & launch

    Move the .jar into .minecraft/mods and launch Minecraft using the Fabric profile. That's it!
